# OpenTalib User Manual

Welcome to **OpenTalib**, your intelligent, multi-agent AI learning platform. This manual provides a comprehensive guide for all user roles: Students, Teachers, and Administrators.

---

## 1. Introduction to OpenTalib

OpenTalib is not just a collection of lessons; it is a dynamic, interactive learning environment. It uses AI "agents" (teachers and students) to bring subjects to life.

### Key Concepts
- **AI Classroom:** An immersive space where an AI teacher explains concepts using slides, discussions, and interactive models.
- **AI Agents:** You aren't alone! AI student agents often participate in discussions, asking questions and sharing insights.
- **Personalized Path:** The system tracks what you know and automatically schedules reviews for concepts you find difficult.

---

## 2. For Students (Learners)

Whether you are a **School Student** (linked to a teacher) or a **Mature Student** (independent learner), your journey starts on your Dashboard.

### 2.1 Your Dashboard
- **Today's Review:** A list of concepts you need to practice today based on the Spaced Repetition system.
- **My Courses:** All courses you have created or been assigned.
- **Mock Exams:** Timed assessments to test your readiness.
- **Study Planner:** A personalized weekly schedule leading up to your real exam dates.

### 2.2 Navigating the AI Classroom
- **Interactive Slides:** Read or listen to the AI teacher's explanation.
- **AI Tutor:** Click the "?" button anytime to ask the AI teacher a private question about the current lesson.
- **Quizzes:** Take quick quizzes at the end of each concept. Your score determines when you next need to review this topic.
- **Revision Cards:** Click the "Revision Cards" button in any course to generate a printable set of flashcards for all key concepts.

### 2.3 Independent Learning (Mature Students only)
As a Mature Student, you can generate your own courses:
1. Click **"New Course"**.
2. Enter a topic (e.g., "Quantum Physics for Beginners").
3. (Optional) Upload a PDF textbook.
4. The AI will build a custom interactive classroom for you.

---

## 3. For Teachers

As a teacher, you manage the curriculum and track student progress.

### 3.1 Managing Your Class
- **Invite Code:** Every teacher has a unique code. Share this with your students so they can link to your dashboard during signup.
- **Student Management:** View your list of students, update their grades, or reset their passwords.

### 3.2 Creating & Assigning Content
- **Course Generation:** Create high-quality, board-aligned courses from prompts or PDFs.
- **Board Alignment:** Choose your exam board (e.g., FBISE, Cambridge) to ensure the AI uses the correct terminology and marking style.
- **Assignments:** Once a course is generated, use the "Assign" button to send it to specific students or your entire class.

### 3.3 Assessment & Analytics
- **Mock Exams:** Create timed exams from any course. You can see student scores and time taken.
- **Heatmaps:** View a "Concept Heatmap" to see which topics your class is struggling with collectively.
- **Activity Log:** Track real-time engagement and quiz attempts.

---

## 4. For Administrators

Administrators oversee the entire platform infrastructure.

### 4.1 Platform Management
- **User Directory:** View all accounts, promote users to the `teacher` role, or disable accounts.
- **Subject Registry:** Add new subjects with custom icons (emojis) that will appear across the platform.
- **System Stats:** Monitor disk usage (for media), total course volume, and user distribution.

---

## 5. Tips for Best Results

### For Course Generation
- **Be Specific:** Instead of "Math", try "Algebraic Fractions for 9th Grade".
- **Use High-Quality PDFs:** If uploading a document, ensure it is clear text (not a blurry scan) for the best AI comprehension.
- **Select the Right Language:** If you want bilingual instruction (e.g., English/Urdu), select the specific language mix in the generation settings.

### For Learning
- **Use the AI Tutor:** Don't just read the slides. Ask "Can you give me another analogy for this?" or "How would this be asked in an exam?".
- **Print Your Cards:** Use the Revision Cards feature to study away from the screen.
- **Follow Your Study Plan:** The Study Planner is designed to prevent cramming. Doing small sessions every day is much more effective than one long session.
